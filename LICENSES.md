## Publishing responsibly

1. Preserve the MIT license/copyright notice in copies of MIT-covered software. The already-published GitHub `LICENSE` says **“Copyright (c) 2026 Sandbox Rise”**; this bundle reproduces it unchanged. The owner should confirm the intended rights-holder spelling before any owner-approved change to that line.
2. When distributing the website's WOFF2 fonts, include their **full family-specific OFL texts and copyright notices**. MIT does not relicense the fonts. [Font provenance and attribution](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) are recorded separately.
3. Do not treat a public profile, logo image or brand story as permission to reuse the SandboxRise identity. Request written permission for brand use; nothing here asserts a registered trademark or a particular legal entity.
4. This repository bundle contains **documentation and one small profile mark**. It does **not** contain the website source, Apps Script backend, font binaries, email kit, a live OTP service or a published client portfolio. If the separate website is published later, carry the applicable notices alongside the files they cover.

This scope explanation is not an attempt to revoke any license already granted for material in earlier commits or distributions. Where a third party's own license applies, its terms take precedence for that material.
